Privacy by design

Built to keep patient data minimal and safe.

Data stays in your browser

This demo has no server. Bookings live in your browser's local storage, and you can clear them any time.

No medical details stored

Aria asks only what it needs to book: name, mobile, visit type, insurance carrier. It tells patients not to share medical details in chat, and saves only the last 4 digits of a member ID.

Consent before texting

Patients tick a consent box before any SMS. STOP opt-outs are honored automatically. A live setup would run on HIPAA-eligible vendors under a signed BAA.

This is a product demo. It is not HIPAA certified, and no certification is claimed.
